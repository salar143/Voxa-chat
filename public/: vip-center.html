❓ VIP Rules & Help

How do I increase VIP EXP?

Every 1,000 recharge gives 100 VIP EXP. VIP EXP increases your VIP level.

What is the VIP period?

Each VIP period lasts 95 days. At the end of every period, EXP resets and a new 95-day period begins.

When can I use a GIF avatar?

GIF Avatar unlocks from VIP 6. VIP 6 and higher users can upload an animated GIF avatar.

What are the VIP levels?

VIP has 10 levels: VIP 1 to VIP 10. Each level unlocks more privileges.

What is included in VIP 10?

VIP 10 includes Crown Bubble, Gold Diamond Frame, Luxury Profile Card, and Royal Entry Effect.
